DeepCalc is a focused calculation workspace for working things out, line by line. It is a native Android app built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

Features:
• Live results beside each expression
• Reusable variables and references to earlier results
• Powers, square roots, percentages and comments
• Separate notes beside calculations
• Calculation fields open the DeepCalc keypad; notes always use the Android keyboard
• Switch calculation keyboard from the header, with automatic focus and scroll handling
• Autosaved active sheet and local History for up to 100 sheets
• Copy Sheet, Copy Complete and Android's native Share menu
• System, Light, Dark and AMOLED themes with five accent palettes

DeepCalc does not request Internet access and contains no ads or analytics SDKs. Calculations, notes and saved sheets stay on your device.
